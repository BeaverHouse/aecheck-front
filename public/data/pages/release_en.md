# Release Notes

Only the changes that matter, recorded from November 2026.

## 2026-11-28

### Check from screenshots

- Upload screenshots of the in-game Menu → Records → Styles screen, and the characters you own are found and checked. You can upload several at once.
- Before anything is checked, you see what was found and can leave any character out.
- ★4 and ★5 share the same picture, so they cannot be told apart. The ★4 is picked; switch it to the ★5 if you own that.
- Characters with a lookalike picture, and characters AE Check does not list, are shown separately.
- Your images never leave your device. The matching runs in your browser.
- The first use can take more than 10 seconds while the character pictures download.

### Check page

- Fewer rows of buttons above the list on phones.
- Each filter is named for what it filters, and opens a list to pick from.
- Grasta steps read No grasta / Grasta equipped / True grasta equipped, and Stellar Awakening reads Awakening Gauge 0/3 to 2/3 / Awakened.
- Actions that change everything at once, such as check all and clear all, are in the ⋯ menu.
- Page navigation moved below the list.
- Fixed the announcement coming back after it was closed.
